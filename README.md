## The pitch

> FerretFlix rents harness-trained ferrets to cable companies, who pay
> $40 a visit to thread fiber through walls no human hand can reach.

The idea sounds like a joke — renting ferrets — until the second look:
cable companies genuinely pay for a small, sure-footed animal that can
thread fiber through wall cavities, ducts, and conduits no human hand
can reach. The customer is clear, the price is clear, and the ferret
is the only tool that fits the job. Each visit is a repeat booking:
every new building needs the same pull, so one trained ferret earns
its keep many times over.
